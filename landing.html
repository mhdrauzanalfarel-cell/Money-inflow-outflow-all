Capabilities

Built around your workflow.

Every feature is designed to reduce friction. You should spend time on your finances, not on your tools.

Wallet auto-scan
Connect any EVM wallet address. Token balances are fetched automatically via Alchemy with no manual input required.
IDR conversion
Live USD/IDR exchange rate fetched on every load. Portfolio values are always displayed in your local currency.
Fiat accounts
Track Dana, GoPay, BCA, cash and other accounts manually. Full picture of net worth across crypto and fiat.
Cashflow analytics
Income and expense breakdowns over 24H, 7D, 1M, and 1Y. Compare periods and understand spending patterns.
Transaction history
Every entry logged with timestamp, category, and amount. Filter by direction, source, or date range.
Cloud sync
Data persists across sessions and devices via Supabase. No local storage dependency. Access from any browser.
How it works

One view. Complete picture.

Track crypto wallets
01
Add any EVM wallet address. Tap scan and all token balances are fetched in seconds — ETH, USDT, custom tokens, everything.
Monitor fiat balances
02
Enter your Dana, bank, or cash balances manually. They sit alongside your crypto in the same dashboard, in IDR.
Log income and expenses
03
Record transactions with a single tap. Categorize as income or expense, add a note, and the analytics update immediately.
Read analytics
04
Switch between 24H, 7D, 1M, and 1Y to understand your cashflow trends. Income vs expense, side by side.
